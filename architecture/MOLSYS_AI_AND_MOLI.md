# MolSys-AI and MOLI

MolSys-AI is optional agent/intelligence infrastructure across MolSysSuite.

MOLI is the primary scientist-facing agent.

## MOLI may
- interpret intent;
- inspect DiscoveryProject graphs;
- propose Questions, Hypotheses, Strategies, Claims and investigations;
- identify uncertainty/conflict;
- invoke DiscoveryEngine;
- invoke Praxis directly;
- use raw Modeling/Sabueso APIs;
- interpret Results, Observations and Evidence;
- help compose experimental workflows.

## Multi-level access
`Discovery ↔ Capability ↔ Protocol ↔ API`

Semantic Capabilities do not hide expert APIs.

## Authority
MOLI's mutation/execution rights are governed by policy/approval gates.

**MOLI proposal ≠ scientific certification.**
