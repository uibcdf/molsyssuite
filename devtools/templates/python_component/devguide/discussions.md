# __COMPONENT_NAME__ Discussions upkeep

Maintainers of `__REPOSITORY__` own triage until explicitly delegated. Use this
repository for its usage questions and resources; actionable work belongs in its
owning issue. Read the common Discussions policy through `MOLSYSSUITE_GUIDE.md`.

- **Announcements:** maintainer updates linking an existing release/decision.
- **Ideas:** early proposals; convert actionable work to an owning issue.
- **Q&A:** usage and interpretation; mark useful answers and link lasting changes.

Retain these three categories, even when empty. Datasets and Benchmarks,
Literature and Resources, and Methods and Tools are individual optional
suggestions. Document purposeful local extensions and forms here after their
owner selects them; this generated guide does not configure GitHub.

Search and reuse threads; include the task, versions, minimal permitted example,
sources and expected/observed result. Keep substantive resource reviews and
scientific arguments in their conversations and link reproducible artifacts.
Maintainers document each actual Discussion label's meaning and responsible
applicator here when labels are selected; no additional label vocabulary is
prescribed by the starter. Categories express purpose; labels are local facets.

Link duplicates and useful answers, preserve history and append dated corrections.
Record accepted/deferred/rejected decisions in the owning issue or versioned
record with reciprocal links, reason and a concrete deferral revisit trigger.
Follow normal disclosure/posting authority; reading grants no notification,
subscription or scientific-implementation authority. Update this guide when
categories, ownership, forms or local labels change.
