## What

scientific agent in future or substantively revised human-facing prose. Preserve
exact technical identifiers, URLs, historical quotations and immutable evidence.
MolSys-AI and its specialist agents remain distinct.

## How

human/system use, and carry the practical action in the mandatory common guide
and official starter. Update maintained central introductory/index prose. Deliver
the byte-identical canonical guide to all seventeen members through the official
synchronizer, with owning advance notices and exact administrative receiving.

## Why

future prose makes that distinction clear without renaming technical interfaces
or turning optional agency into a requirement.
