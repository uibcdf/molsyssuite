# MolSysSuite Discussions upkeep

The maintainers of `uibcdf/molsyssuite` (dprada/LMMV until delegated) own triage.
Use [this area's conversations](https://github.com/uibcdf/molsyssuite/discussions)
for shared suite subjects. Member usage belongs with its member; platform-wide
work belongs to MolI. There is no response-time promise.

The common required baseline is Announcements (maintainer updates linking an
existing release/decision), Ideas (early proposals) and Q&A (usage/interpretation,
with useful answers marked). Datasets and Benchmarks, Literature and Resources,
and Methods and Tools are individual optional suggestions. This guide does not
assert that any suggested category or UI form is configured. Document a selected
local extension's purpose/audience, owner, forms and durable destination here.

Search/reuse existing conversations and cite precise sources, dates and versions.
Resource reviews, evidence, scientific arguments and conclusions stay in their
conversations; link their reproducible artifacts and authoritative issue/decision.
Include enough permitted context for another contributor to understand the task.

Maintainers select/apply useful local Discussion labels and document their
purpose here as introduced. Categories express purpose; labels are facets.
The bug/proposal/documentation and state vocabulary in `reporting_protocol.md`
governs issues; it is not an invented additional Discussion-label requirement.

Link duplicates instead of deleting useful history. Mark useful answers; append
dated corrections and keep ongoing resource conversations reusable. Convert
actionable findings to the owning issue. Record accepted/deferred/rejected
decisions with date/reason and a concrete revisit trigger in the owning issue or
versioned record; link the conversation and destination in both directions.

Follow normal confidentiality and posting authority. Reading grants no posting,
notifications, subscription or scientific-implementation authority. Configuration,
source forms, guide delivery and rendered UI behavior require separate evidence.
Review these instructions when categories, forms, ownership or label meanings
change. Full policy: [github_discussions.md](github_discussions.md).
