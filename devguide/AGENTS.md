# MolSysSuite developer-guide instructions

Read [../AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) and [reporting_protocol.md](reporting_protocol.md)
before editing issue-backed records. Current policy and relevant active queues
are ordinary reading; use archive indexes for orientation and open historical
records for a stated question. Archive resolved records and regenerate their
indexes in the same change. Preserve original archived claims and append dated
corrections. Run the offline governance guard before committing.

## Durable working instructions

Keep technical findings in owning issues, tests and maintained documents. Place
accepted lasting directory-specific actions here, repository-wide actions at
root, and route shared proposals through
[the canonical policy summary](../MOLSYSSUITE_GUIDE.md#durable-working-instructions).
