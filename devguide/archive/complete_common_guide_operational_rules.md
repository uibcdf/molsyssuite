## How

maintained documents. Add an action-trigger navigation map, preserve existing
anchors and distinguish applicability, pending adoption and bounded exceptions.
Explain guide commit/hash identity, live normative links and frozen automated
callers separately. Make supported reusable input/output, unit/index, failure
and compatibility contracts explicit without changing component science.

Publish the provider, then use the registered official synchronizer to deliver
only the common guide in task-owned member clones. Retain advance owner notices,
published source identities, exact applicable gates and live committed-input
receipts separately. Byte-copy adoption uses the central issue; no independent
member design, exception or new local issue is needed unless a real gap emerges.

New contributors can otherwise choose an unresolved CI route, overwrite the
application's units or duplicate shared root rules despite green link checks.
The accepted correction improves discoverability and semantic consistency;
structural checks alone do not prove contributor behavior or scientific validity.
