# Appendix B — Operational / Epistemic Stress Tests



### 1. Two contradictory hypotheses
Both may coexist and share/compete for Evidence.
**Required:** graph relations and non-exclusive hypotheses. PASS.

### 2. Two validated Protocols disagree
Store both Runs/Results; do not overwrite. Evidence can conflict.
**Required:** Protocol identity/version, provenance, conflict representation. PASS with specification pressure.

### 3. Experiment invalidates an earlier Decision
New Decision may supersede old Decision; old rationale/history remains.
**Required:** supersession/history. PASS after refinement.

### 4. Capability fails mid-Campaign
Campaign remains partially completed; failed Run is first-class.
**Required:** failure/partial status. PASS.

### 5. Protocol version changes
Past Run points to exact Protocol version; new Campaign may use new version.
**Required:** immutable/versioned execution reference. PASS.

### 6. Independent reproduction
A second Run/Experiment can explicitly reproduce/challenge prior Result/Claim.
**Required:** relations such as reproduces/challenges may be useful. PASS; relation vocabulary open.

### 7. MOLI proposes a bad Strategy
Human rejects it; proposal/rejection may be recorded without execution.
**Required:** approval/authority boundary. PASS.

### 8. Campaign is inconclusive
Inconclusive is not failure and must remain scientifically meaningful.
**Required:** explicit inconclusive status/outcome. PASS.

### 9. Observation remains unexplained for years
Observation persists without forced Hypothesis.
**Required:** Observation independence. PASS.

### 10. Candidate shared by two projects
Entity identity may be shared; Candidate role/state is project contextual.
**Required:** stable references and project-specific roles. PASS.

### 11. Project bifurcates
A project may branch or spawn related projects while preserving lineage.
**Required:** project lineage/fork relation. PASS; exact model open.

### 12. External knowledge changes later
Past Decision must retain the external knowledge snapshot/reference/version used at decision time; current Sabueso knowledge may differ.
**Required:** temporal/versioned external knowledge references. PASS with important requirement.

## New concept under test: Claim
Operational tests suggest value in an explicit assertion object between Observation/Result and higher-level scientific reasoning, especially for reproduction/challenge relations. However, it may duplicate other concepts. Keep provisional until concrete Nextia schemas/examples demonstrate necessity.

## Overall
Stress testing strengthens rather than changes the four-column architecture. Remaining pressure is mainly on Nextia/Praxis contracts, versioning, relations and history.
