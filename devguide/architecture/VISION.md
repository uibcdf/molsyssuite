# MolSysSuite — Vision

MolSysSuite is a unified ecosystem for computational molecular science and molecular discovery, not merely a collection of libraries.

| Column | Fundamental question | Primary component(s) |
|---|---|---|
| Knowledge | What is known? | Sabueso |
| Modeling | What can we represent, model, compute, analyze and visualize? | MolSysMT, MolSysViewer, TopoMT, ElastNetMT, PharmacophoreMT, DockingMT, ... |
| Capabilities | What do we know how to do reproducibly? | Praxis |
| Discovery | What are we trying to discover, how do we investigate it, and what have we learned? | Nextia |

A useful mnemonic is **KNOW → MODEL → DO → DISCOVER → LEARN**. This is a conceptual cycle, not a mandatory pipeline.

Discovery creates two durable forms of value: **scientific learning** (new knowledge) and **methodological learning** (new validated capabilities).

MolSys-AI is an optional intelligent layer across the four columns; MOLI is its primary scientist-facing agent. The scientific stack must remain complete without an LLM.

The architecture is broader than drug discovery and should support small molecules, peptides, proteins, enzymes, probes, biosensors, biocatalysts and other molecular engineering/discovery objectives.
